# Advanced AI Applications in Manufacturing & Semiconductor Systems

**Author:** Randall C. Crawford

This repository contains **four technical presentations** examining the application of **reinforcement learning (RL), explainable AI (XAI), and digital twin technologies** across advanced manufacturing and semiconductor domains. Collectively, these works focus on improving **quality assurance, optimization, safety, scalability, and trust** in complex industrial systems.

---

## 1. Collaborative Multi-Robot Weld Inspection with Reinforcement Learning

**Author:** Randall C. Crawford
**Presentation File:**
`ANA505_Assignment1_Presentation_9-7-2025.pptx`

**Domain:** Automotive manufacturing
**Focus:** Safe reinforcement learning, multi-robot coordination, human oversight

This presentation introduces a **collaborative multi-robot inspection system** for automotive weld quality assurance. Reinforcement learning enables adaptive defect detection and coordinated task allocation across multiple robots, while **controller-based safety mechanisms and human-in-the-loop oversight** ensure zero safety incidents. The system demonstrates improved detection accuracy, reduced cycle time, and robust performance under stochastic production interruptions.

**Key topics:**

* Multi-robot coordination with RL
* Safe RL using controller-based constraints
* Human–robot collaboration for real-time monitoring
* Quantified gains in defect detection and efficiency

## 2. Reinforcement Learning Optimization for Advanced Chip Physical Design (2nm)

**Author:** Randall C. Crawford
**Presentation File:**
`ANA505_Assignment2_Presentation_9-14-2025.pptx`

**Domain:** Semiconductor physical design
**Focus:** RL-driven optimization across the full design flow

This presentation explores the application of reinforcement learning to **advanced-node (2nm) semiconductor physical design**, covering partitioning, floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and timing closure. The work highlights the integration of **RL with neural guidance and Benders Decomposition**, demonstrating improvements in runtime, congestion reduction, and timing slack.

**Key topics:**

* RL-based partitioning and floorplanning
* Neural-guided placement and CTS
* Congestion and timing optimization
* Scalable optimization for advanced semiconductor nodes

## 3. Factory Digital Twins with NVIDIA Omniverse

**Author:** Randall C. Crawford
**Presentation File:**
`ANA505_Assignment3_Presentation_9-21-2025.pptx`

**Domain:** Smart manufacturing systems
**Focus:** Digital twins, AI integration, secure workflows

This presentation examines **factory digital twins built on NVIDIA Omniverse**, integrating simulation, synthetic data, AI/ML models, robotics, and IoT pipelines. Emphasis is placed on **secure IT architectures**, OpenUSD-based interoperability, and AI-driven workflow optimization, positioning digital twins as a foundational capability for modern, connected manufacturing environments.

**Key topics:**

* End-to-end factory digital twin architecture
* Simulation and synthetic data for AI workflows
* Secure deployment and delivery pipelines
* Scalable collaboration across manufacturing systems

## 4. Explainable AI for Defect Detection in Manufacturing Vision Systems

**Author:** Randall C. Crawford
**Presentation File:**
`ANA505_Assignment4_Presentation_9-28-2025.pptx`

**Domain:** Semiconductor manufacturing & computer vision
**Focus:** Explainability, trust, and scalable AI

This presentation investigates **Explainable AI (XAI)** techniques applied to computer vision-based defect detection, with a focus on wafer inspection. Methods such as **Grad-CAM, SHAP, LIME, and interpretable time-series models** are shown to improve classification, localization, segmentation, and periodic defect detection, addressing the performance–explainability trade-off in high-stakes manufacturing environments.

**Key topics:**

* XAI for defect classification and localization
* Interpretability in time-series defect detection
* Multi-feature fusion with explainable models
* Trust and deployment readiness of AI systems

## Overall Themes & Takeaways

Across all four presentations, the work emphasizes:

* **Reinforcement learning** as a unifying optimization framework
* **Safety, trust, and explainability** as first-class design constraints
* **Human–AI collaboration** in industrial environments
* **Scalability** from robotic cells to semiconductor fabs and digital factories

Together, these presentations demonstrate how advanced AI techniques can be responsibly deployed to improve **efficiency, quality, and reliability** in next-generation manufacturing and semiconductor systems.
